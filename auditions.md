# The audition book — every transform, heard

The claim this doc exists to keep honest: every pronunciation transform
Moira applies has an audible exhibit here, or a pointer to one. The
golden pins the text; this doc pins the sound. The ear re-auditions any
class after a flattener change. Spoken: `just moira-auditions`.

## Rules — the flattener's amortized patterns

The date rule speaks the current year without a year: written 2026-09-14,
and keeps the year when it is not now: written 2020-03-01. The clock rule
is the 24-hour register: the session logged 14:32 and resumed 09:01.
The task rule: td-1692ee and td-8ece4e. The decision rule: ADR-028 and
ADR-013. Capital acronyms stay letters — TTS, SSH, TUI — unless the
lexicon has earned a word: ASCII. The title rule: the Rev. Rupert Bingham. The path rule speaks the basename:
briefs/2026-08-29-brief-terminal-speech.md and src/render.ts. The
glyphs carry: the arrow → means to, and a mid dot · becomes a comma.
