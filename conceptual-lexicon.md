# Conceptual Lexicon — Blandings's terms of art

The silo's own terms of art. A term another silo could also use belongs in
`system.md` instead — the test is *could another silo use this term?* Cited terms
in briefs and decisions resolve here.

**The rule:** every entry **cites the document that defines it**. No invented
definitions. A term with no single defining document names the document that
uses it most precisely, and says so.
